How the AI agent plans Sam’s farm

It runs its own research: picks tools, reads the results, decides what to check next, then reviews and fixes its own work.

1

Sam’s land and goals: income, part-time, under $5,000

2

Calls 1 of 9 tools: frost, soil, slope, NASA, land use, forecast, crops

4

Reads the real data and loops back to decide, up to 12 rounds

5

Reflect

A fact-checker tests the plan; the agent rewrites what was wrong

Venkateshwaran: This is the agentic part. The agent gets Sam’s land and goals as its goal. It decides which tool to call next and gives a reason, shown live on screen. It calls one of nine tools that fetch real data, reads the result, and decides what to check next, for up to twelve rounds. If it tries to write the plan before every core check is done, the system sends it back. Then a fact-checker tests its plan against the data, and the agent corrects its own mistakes.
